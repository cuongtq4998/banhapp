# Admin Orders: Non-functional, Dependencies, Assumptions, Sources (sections 8 to 11)

Part of [Admin Orders](../admin-orders.md) (PRD v1.3). Holds: Non-functional, Impact & Dependencies, Assumptions A-1 to A-11, Research & Sources S-1 to S-6. Changed or new lines end with [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] or [v1.3 | 2026-10-31].

## 8. Non-functional

- Language: Vietnamese for all text. Dates as dd/MM/yyyy and money in whole dong with dot thousands separators, as in Place Order FR-21.
- Platforms: iOS and Android, built at the same time (D-2); native look as in the customer app. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]
- Security: sign-in is the only way to see customer data. Responses to the sign-in screen do not reveal whether an address is registered (FR-1). Code requests are limited to stop abuse (BR-5). Codes and session credentials are not stored in readable form. A reused renewal credential signs the device out (FR-36). Every settings and status action is authorised on the server (FR-5, FR-21). Owner recovery needs two mailboxes (BR-17). Accepted risk (Q-18): ordinary owner sign-in needs one code, so control of the owner mailbox is enough to sign in as owner; the recovery flow is the only one that warns both mailboxes. The store-review account has a fixed code and is enabled only around store review (BR-22). [v1.3 | 2026-10-31]
- Privacy: staff see customer name, phone and address, which is personal data. Access is limited to active accounts (BR-15), to orders of the last 90 days and older unfinished orders shown only in the Unfinished view (BR-20, BR-27) [v1.4 | 2026-10-31], the data is not placed in notifications, logs or analytics, and the privacy notice and store declaration of the admin app state this use (see PO NFR on privacy). Staff and owner e-mail addresses are personal data too and are used only for sign-in and the notices of FR-43. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]
- Performance (proposed). "Normal mobile connection" means 4G with at least 5 Mbit/s and a round trip of at most 300 ms. Measurable on the server alone: the list page (50 orders, 90 days of data, about 100 orders a day) is answered within 1 second and a status change within 2 seconds in 95 percent of requests. On the phone: first rows within 3 seconds and the result of a status change within 5 seconds on the connection above, otherwise an error with retry shows. A sign-in code e-mail is handed to the mail service within 60 seconds; arrival in the mailbox depends on the provider and is not tested. The 60 seconds of G-1 and the 5 minutes of BR-8 are server-side bounds as defined there. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31]
- Accessibility: text scales with the system font size; every control has a text label; contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 body text); status is never shown by colour alone (each status has its word).
- Cost: no text-message cost (decided, Q-3). The new running costs are e-mail delivery, which is low at this volume (S-5), and the store records of the admin app (D-1). Push services are expected to be free at this volume (unverified, left to the architect, TQ-3). Money decision (Q-21, owner, 2026-10-31): going live on paid hosting is not approved; the free tier is used first. Nothing in this document may be read as approval to pay. On free tiers the goals G-1 and BR-8, the daily mail cap and availability are best effort (a free service may sleep or throttle); the owner decides on paid hosting before real customers rely on the app. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31]

## 9. Impact & Dependencies

- Place Order: reads the same orders. Its API changes in four ways: settings change what it returns (catalog, availability, lead days, minimum cakes); cancellation now exists, which makes PO BR-9 testable end to end; the catalog may hold more than four cakes with owner-supplied images (the customer app was designed for at most four cakes with fixed photos), so its catalog screen and its rules text must follow BR-18 and BR-21; and the per-phone limit changes as below. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]
- Cross-feature impact, decided Q-12: Place Order Q-O3 is answered. A cancelled order does not count toward the limit of 5 orders per phone per 24 hours, except one cancelled with the reason "fake or spam order". The place-order backend rule must change (FR-44, BR-16); Place Order BR and ACs that state the limit need an update by that feature's owner. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]
- Place Order Q-O4 (retention period for contact data) applies to stored data and stays open by owner decision (decide later, not blocking); staff see only the last 90 days (BR-20), which is a display rule, not a retention period. Until a period is decided, stored customer data is not deleted; the owner should decide before real customers use the shop (security and customer data). [v1.3 | 2026-10-31]
- Place Order PRD v1.6 records the changes of this document: FR-44 rule for cancelled orders (new PO BR-14, AC-35 to AC-37, Q-O3 answered) and up to 12 cakes instead of at most four (PO FR-3, BR-13, AC-4). [v1.3 | 2026-10-31]
- Backend: new endpoints for sign-in, devices, orders and settings (designed by the architect); the database already guards status changes and releases capacity on cancellation (docs/system/order-lifecycle.md); a cancel reason and removed-cake state need new stored data. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]
- docs/system/auth.md was rewritten for e-mail code sign-in (v2.0); the architect's ADR-0022 (sessions, 20-second grace) and ADR-0027 (review account) are accepted by the owner in these respects. The architect aligns recovery counters (separate from ordinary sign-in, BR-17) and the no-time-lock rule (BR-5), see TQ-11. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31]
- Design: reference screens A1 to A4 exist for iOS and Android; they are reference only and this document is authoritative. They show a phone-number sign-in, a 30-second resend wait and a "remembered N days" note, which must change to e-mail and 7 days; they show no device list, backup e-mail, cake add or remove, cancel reason or ordering rules. The UI specification docs/design/admin-orders/ (20 screens) replaces them. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31]
- External: an e-mail delivery service (low cost, S-5), Apple and Google push services, Zalo and the phone dialer. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]

## 10. Assumptions

- A-1: Shop timezone is Vietnam (UTC+7); "today" and "tomorrow" use the server date in that zone (Place Order A-1, A-8).
- A-2: There is one shop. The first owner account is created by the system operator at launch, outside the app; the app has no self-registration.
- A-3: Staff are few (up to 30) and all work for the same shop; any staff member may act on any order. No per-staff assignment. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]
- A-4: Each person signs in on one to three personal devices; these devices have mobile data and can receive e-mail. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]
- A-5: The shop handles at most about 100 orders a day; list size and push volume are small.
- A-6: Orders are handled by phone or Zalo outside the app; no in-app messaging with customers.
- A-7: If the owner has no backup e-mail, or loses access to both mailboxes, the operator recovers the account outside the app. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]
- A-8: The code is sent by e-mail; the sender and wording are chosen by the architect. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31]
- A-9: Sample and test data are fake.
- A-10: Values marked "proposed" in BR-5, BR-8, BR-9, BR-13, BR-18, BR-23 to BR-25 and the performance figures are defaults chosen by the author; the owner may change them. A changed value is written into this document first (new version and change log), so documents and tests follow one source. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31]
- A-11: Apple and Google reviewers can use the fixed-code review account for the whole review (S-3, unverified), and the operator switches it off after approval. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31]
