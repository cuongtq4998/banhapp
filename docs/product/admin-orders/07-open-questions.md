## 12. Open Questions

Q-1 to Q-14 were answered by the owner on 2026-10-31 and are closed. Q-15 to Q-17 were decided by the owner on 2026-10-31 and are closed. Q-18, Q-19 and Q-21 are new owner decisions (v1.3).
