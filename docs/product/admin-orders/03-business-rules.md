## 6. Business Rules

| ID | Rule |
| -- | ---- |
| BR-1 | Rights. Owner: everything. Staff (and the review account): sign in and out, list, search, open, confirm, complete and cancel orders, receive notifications, sign out own other phones. Only the owner: Settings (FR-20 to FR-24) and staff (FR-5). Neither role can edit an order, delete an order or see payment data. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-2 | Process (owner 2026-11-02): Received, Processing (about 1 day), Delivery date scheduled (expected date only), Delivery started, Finished; Cancelled is the other end. Status changes, and no others: Received to Processing, Received to Cancelled, Processing to Delivery date scheduled (expected date entered, FR-28), Processing to Cancelled, Delivery date scheduled to Delivery started (real fee entered, FR-29), Delivery started to Finished. Cancel is not possible from Delivery date scheduled onward (the cake may have no recipient). Finished and Cancelled are final; the real fee may be corrected only while Delivery started (BR-17), not after Finished. [v1.12 | 2026-11-02] Cancelling returns the cakes to the remaining amount of the requested date at once (PO BR-9); no other change moves capacity (BR-15). Superseded 2026-11-02: four changes New to Confirmed, New to Cancelled, Confirmed to Completed, Confirmed to Cancelled (v1.5). [v1.10 | 2026-11-02] |
| BR-3 | Record (invisible). The system stores, and nobody can edit or delete, every order creation and status change (old and new status, account name or "customer", time, cancel reason) and every settings change (account name, field, old and new value, time). Only the last change of an order is shown (FR-14); no history or audit screen (deferred). A deactivated staff member keeps the name in earlier records. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-4 | Codes (invisible, approved defaults). A code has 6 digits, works once, and is valid for less than 10 minutes (at exactly 10:00 it is expired). A new code can be requested after 30 seconds and voids the old one; at most 3 codes per address per hour. The 5th wrong entry voids the current code. There is no time lock on the address (Q-20), and a signed-in phone is never affected. An unregistered address behaves the same and gets no mail. Only addresses the owner added can sign in; letter case and spaces around are ignored; an address needs "@" with text on both sides and a dot after it, at most 254 characters, and belongs to one account. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-5 | Sessions (invisible, Q-4, Q-15). A phone stays signed in; each use renews it for 7 days, so a phone used weekly never asks again; at 7 days without use a code is needed. Renewal credentials work once; an old one presented more than 20 seconds after replacement signs that phone out; within 20 seconds the repeat succeeds (ADR-0022). Access to data uses a credential of at most 5 minutes, which is why a removed phone loses data within 5 minutes. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-6 | Accounts (Q-5). One owner and up to 30 active staff; adding or switching on a staff member is refused at 30 with a message to switch one off first. The owner cannot be switched off. The review account (BR-14) is not counted. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-7 | Customer data (invisible). Visible only to a signed-in active account; not in notification text, logs, crash reports or analytics (the admin app has no analytics); removed from the phone at sign-out; hidden in the phone's app preview; not part of phone backup. Retention follows PO BR-12 and stays open (PO Q-O4). [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-8 | Cancel reasons (Q-9, list shortened by Q-28, owner 2026-10-31). One required reason: customer asked, cannot deliver (also when the day is full), cannot reach customer, fake or spam order. Shown to staff and owner, never to the customer. A cancelled order does not count toward the 5-per-phone limit, except "fake or spam" (Q-12). The four reasons stay unchanged; they can be chosen only while the order is Received or Processing (BR-2). [v1.10 | 2026-11-02] |
| BR-9 | What the list shows. List date = the expected delivery date when one is set, otherwise the requested date the customer chose (BR-15; owner decision 2026-11-02, Q-37). [v1.11 | 2026-11-02] Unfinished = an open order (Received, Processing, Delivery date scheduled, Delivery started; assumption Q-36) with list date today or earlier (shop calendar, Vietnam time). The default list holds all open orders of any date and any age, ordered by earliest list date, then earliest slot, then oldest placed. Finished and Cancelled orders are shown for 90 days after they were placed (Q-13, owner too; at exactly 90 days still shown), then not listed, searched or opened. No automatic closing. Orders load 50 at a time. The notification is only a prompt; the list is the truth. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-10 | Search. At least 3 characters (3 digits for a phone); "contains", ignoring case and accents ("tran" finds "Trần"); order code with or without "BT-"; phone digits only ("+84 900..." equals "0900..."); combines with filters. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-11 | Shop values (approved default ranges). Price: whole dong 1 to 100 000 000. District estimated fee (shown to customers as an estimate, never charged by itself): whole dong 1 to 10 000 000. Real delivery fee (entered per order when delivery starts, FR-29): whole dong 0 to 10 000 000, 0 means free delivery (Q-33; the upper bound is the same approved default); required, nothing is saved without it; it may be corrected while the order is Delivery started, same range (BR-17). [v1.12 | 2026-11-02] Superseded 2026-11-02: "Delivery fee 1 to 10 000 000, 0 refused" (v1.7, Q-29). Daily limit 1 to 9 999. Days of notice 0 to 14. Minimum cakes 1 to 20 and not above the daily limit, checked on the saved pair. Slot: start before end, no duplicate. Texts as in data-model section 3. Contact phone: a Vietnamese number as in PO ADR-0009; Zalo: such a number or an account name of 3 to 100 characters. All fields required; an invalid value is refused with a message naming the field and nothing of that form is saved. Changes apply to orders placed afterwards; existing orders keep their cake, price, district and slot, and the real fee once entered (a changed district estimate never alters an order). [v1.10 | 2026-11-02] Lowering the daily limit cancels nothing: dates above it show as full. The last active cake, district and slot cannot be switched off or removed. Two owner phones saving the same value: the last save wins. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-12 | Cakes. At most 12 cakes not removed (Q-17). Name 1 to 100 characters, unique; one image, JPEG or PNG, at most 5 MB (5 242 880 bytes, approved default); an iPhone HEIC photo is converted to JPEG on the phone first. Hidden cakes can be shown again; removed cakes cannot, are hidden from customers, and stay in earlier orders with their stored name and price. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-13 | New-device notice (invisible, Q-24, backup address part deferred). After an owner code sign-in on a phone on which the owner has no signed-in session at that moment, one notice goes to the owner e-mail: owner account signed in on a new phone, phone model, time, advice to use "Sign out all other phones" if it was not the owner. The owner signing out and then in again on the same phone does get a notice. No code, no link that signs in, no customer data. Not sent for renewals or staff. [v1.7 | 2026-11-01] |
| BR-14 | Review account (invisible, Q-19). A staff account with a fixed address and code, on only while the operator switched it on for store review. It sees only three fake demo orders; real orders never appear for it, demo orders never for others, never count toward limits and never notify. The code is not written in the repository or documents. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-15 | Expected date, fee and total (owner 2026-11-02; new in v1.10). The customer-chosen date stays on the order as the requested date and alone holds capacity against the daily limit (PO BR-2, PO BR-9; assumption Q-36). The expected delivery date is information only: it is not checked against the daily limit, lead days or slots, and moving it never changes any date's remaining amount (owner decision 2026-11-02, Q-38: the limit stays on the customer-chosen date). [v1.11 | 2026-11-02] It must be a valid calendar date, from the shop's today up to today + 7 days inclusive, never in the past; it may be moved any number of times, each new date within the same range of the day it is entered (owner 2026-11-02: "at most 1 week"; confirmed by the owner 2026-11-02, Q-40) [v1.13 | 2026-11-02]. Approved (owner answer 2026-11-02), 7-day rule confirmed 2026-11-02. [v1.13 | 2026-11-02] Superseded 2026-11-02: "not before the shop's today" with no upper limit. [v1.12 | 2026-11-02] Every entry and change is recorded: account name, time, old date (none at first entry) and new date (BR-3). The customer sees the district fee only as an estimate with the text that the final fee is told at delivery. The order stores the cake total, and the real fee once entered; the customer pays the cakes plus the real fee when the cakes are handed over (Q-30, Q-31). The customer is not messaged automatically when the date moves; staff call (assumption Q-35). [v1.10 | 2026-11-02] |
| BR-16 | Refusal at the door (owner 2026-11-02, Q-39; new in v1.12). If the customer refuses the cakes at the door after cancel is locked (from Delivery date scheduled), nothing changes in the app: the case is handled outside the app by the shop. Stated limit: the app has no cancel after "Delivery date scheduled", no "refused" status and no refusal reason; the order is closed as Finished or stays open as staff decide, and the app does not model the loss. Approved (owner answer 2026-11-02). [v1.12 | 2026-11-02] |
| BR-17 | Correcting the real fee (owner 2026-11-02, Q-41; new in v1.12; before v1.12 no correction was defined, so a wrong fee stayed). Staff and owner may correct the real delivery fee while the order is Delivery started, until Finished, any number of times, in whole dong 0 to 10 000 000 (BR-11). Every correction is recorded: account name, time, old fee, new fee (BR-3). After Finished the fee cannot be changed by anyone. No correction in any other status. The customer-facing amount to collect follows the latest fee. Approved (owner answer 2026-11-02). [v1.12 | 2026-11-02] |
