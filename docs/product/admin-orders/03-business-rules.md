# Admin Orders: Business Rules (section 6)

Part of [Admin Orders](../admin-orders.md) (PRD v1.4). Holds: BR-1 to BR-28. Lines changed in v1.4 end with [v1.4 | 2026-10-31]. Values marked "proposed" are defaults the owner may change (see [07-open-questions.md](07-open-questions.md)); a changed value is first changed in this document (new version) and then followed by system documents and tests (A-10). Place Order rules (BR-1 to BR-14 there) are cited as "PO BR-n". Changed or new lines end with [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] or [v1.3 | 2026-10-31].

## 6. Business Rules

| ID | Rule |
| -- | ---- |
| BR-1 | Rights. Owner: everything. Staff (also the store-review account, BR-22): sign in and out, list, search, filter and open orders, confirm, complete and cancel orders, receive notifications, see and revoke own devices. Only the owner: settings (FR-22 to FR-26, FR-38 to FR-40), staff management (FR-27), backup e-mail and recovery (FR-42, FR-43), revoking other people's devices (FR-37). Neither role can edit an order's content, delete an order, or see payment data. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-2 | Allowed status changes, and no others: New to Confirmed, New to Cancelled, Confirmed to Completed, Confirmed to Cancelled. Completed may be set at any time once the order is Confirmed, also before the delivery date (Q-8, decided). Completed and Cancelled are final: no change out of them, no reopening. This follows the lifecycle already enforced by the database (docs/system/order-lifecycle.md section 6). [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-3 | Cancelling an order returns its cakes to the remaining amount of its delivery date at once (PO BR-9). Confirming or completing changes no capacity. |
| BR-4 | Every status change records who made it and when, and for a cancellation the reason (BR-16). The record cannot be edited or deleted. Recording is a Must (FR-45); showing it is FR-20. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-5 | One-time code: 6 digits, valid 10 minutes (exact edge in BR-23), usable once; a new code makes the old one invalid. A new code can be requested 30 seconds after the previous one. At most 3 codes per e-mail address in any 60 minutes. Wrong entries count against the current code: the 5th wrong entry voids that code, so even its correct digits are then refused, and the person is told to request a new code. There is no time lock on the address (decided by the owner, Q-20, option a) [v1.4 | 2026-10-31]: a new code can be requested after the 30-second wait, within the 3 per hour, and starts a fresh count of 5, so a stranger can try at most 15 wrong codes per address per hour. A stranger who knows the owner's address can void codes and use up the 3 requests, but cannot lock the owner out for good: devices already signed in are never affected (FR-6), the owner's recovery flow has its own counters (BR-17), and the operator can recover the account (A-7). An address that is not registered behaves the same way (nothing is sent, FR-1). Limits stay although e-mail is cheap, to stop abuse and mail flooding of a third party. All values proposed. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-6 | Session (Q-4, decided). A device stays signed in for 7 days; each use within that time renews it for another 7 days (decided, Q-15). A device not used for 7 days needs a new code. Signing out ends the session at once on that device. A renewal credential works once; presenting an already used one more than 20 seconds after it was replaced signs that device out (FR-36); within 20 seconds the repeat succeeds (lost response, ADR-0022, accepted by the owner). Access to data uses a short-lived credential of 5 minutes at most. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-7 | Only an e-mail address that the owner added can sign in. Addresses are compared without regard to upper or lower case and without spaces at the start or end. An address belongs to one account, and the backup e-mail of the owner cannot also be an account address. Format: BR-25. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-8 | Accounts (Q-5, decided): exactly one owner and up to 30 active staff; adding a new staff account and reactivating a deactivated one are both refused when 30 are already active, with a message that says to deactivate one first. The store-review account (BR-22) is not counted. The owner cannot deactivate or delete the owner account. A deactivated staff account keeps its name in the status history of earlier orders. When the owner deactivates an account or revokes a device, the device loses access to data within 5 minutes (bound, measured from the owner's action to the last data response; the access credential lives 5 minutes, ADR-0022) and cannot renew its sign-in from that moment. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-9 | Setting limits. Cake price: whole dong, 1 to 100 000 000. Delivery fee: whole dong, at least 1 (PO BR-3, no free delivery), at most 10 000 000. Daily limit: whole number from 1 to 9 999. Lead days: whole number from 0 to 14 (proposed). Minimum cakes per order: whole number from 1 to 20 and not above the daily limit (proposed); this holds after every save, so a daily limit below the current minimum is refused (the message names the field and says to lower the minimum first), a minimum above the current limit is refused, and when both are changed in one form the pair is checked together. Slot: start earlier than end, no two slots with the same start and end, same list on every date (PO BR-10, BR-11). Name and description lengths: as in docs/system/data-model section 3 (name 1 to 100, description 1 to 300, greeting 1 to 300, district name 1 to 100, unique). Contact phone and Zalo contact: formats in BR-25. All fields are required. Two owner devices saving the same setting: the last save wins and the earlier value stays in the record of FR-30 (A-D2). [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-10 | Lowering the daily limit below the cakes already accepted for a date cancels nothing: the existing orders stay, that date shows as full to customers, and cancelling orders on it frees room again. Before saving, the owner is told which dates are already above the new limit (count and dates). Raising the limit makes room at once. |
| BR-11 | Cakes. The owner can add, hide and remove cakes (Q-6, decided). A hidden cake can be shown again; a removed cake cannot. At least one cake must stay active (PO BR-13 treats zero active cakes as an error state of the customer app), so the last active cake cannot be hidden or removed. Likewise the last active district and the last active slot cannot be deactivated. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-12 | Price and fee changes follow PO BR-5 and BR-6: a customer who has an order open on the review step with the old price is asked to confirm the new total (PO behaviour "price changed"); stored orders never change. Hidden or removed cakes, deactivated districts and slots disappear from the customer app at its next refresh and are refused by the server at once. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-13 | List order and size. Default list: New and Confirmed orders, earliest delivery date first, then earliest slot, then oldest placed first. With status Completed or Cancelled in the filter, the same order applies. The list loads 50 orders at a time and loads the next 50 when the person reaches the end. |
| BR-14 | Notification content and reach: see FR-31, FR-32. The notification is a prompt, not the record: the list is always the source of truth, and an order whose notification was lost is still in the list. |
| BR-15 | Customer data in the admin app is visible only to a signed-in active account, is not written to the notification text, logs or crash reports, is not sent to any analytics or advertising tool (the admin app has no analytics; if one is added later, it receives no customer data), and is removed from the device when the person signs out. "Removed" includes the preview the phone shows in its app switcher: screens with customer data are blank or blurred there, and the stored data is not part of a phone backup. A screenshot taken by the person is outside this rule. Retention of the stored data follows PO BR-12; the retention period is still open (PO Q-O4, owner: decide later) and the 90 days of BR-20 are a display rule only, not a retention period. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-16 | Cancel reasons (Q-9, decided). The reason is required and is one of: customer asked, day full, cannot deliver, cannot reach customer, fake or spam order. The reason is shown to staff and owner, not to the customer. A cancelled order does not count toward the limit of 5 orders per phone number per 24 hours (Q-12, decided), except a "fake or spam order" cancellation, which keeps counting. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-17 | Owner recovery (Q-14, decided, two-code form decided in Q-16). Ordinary owner sign-in needs one code (Q-18, decided); recovery is a separate, slower flow of two different codes, one sent to the owner e-mail and one to the backup e-mail, both valid under BR-5 and both entered. It has its own counters and limits (30 seconds between requests, 3 per 60 minutes, 5 wrong entries void that recovery attempt and both its codes), separate from ordinary sign-in, so that a blocked or voided ordinary sign-in does not block recovery and the reverse. The way into recovery is always shown on the sign-in screen; without a registered backup e-mail (or for any address that is not the owner's) the flow looks the same, nothing is sent and every code entry fails like a wrong code (FR-1, Q-D1). The operator then recovers the account outside the app (A-7). Each successful recovery sends one notice to both mailboxes. The owner is reminded in the app to register a backup e-mail until it is set. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-18 | New cake data. Name 1 to 100 characters and unique among cakes not removed; description 1 to 300; price as in BR-9; exactly one image, JPEG or PNG, of at most 5 MB = 5 242 880 bytes (proposed; a file of exactly that size is accepted, one byte more is refused). A photo in HEIC format (the usual iPhone format) is converted to JPEG on the phone before sending, and the size limit applies to the converted file; the server accepts only JPEG and PNG. At most 12 cakes not removed, active or hidden (decided, Q-17); adding a 13th is refused (FR-38, AC-61). [v1.2 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-19 | Removing a cake. Removed means hidden from the customer app, refused for new orders, and kept in the database for history. Earlier orders keep the cake name, quantity and price as stored and show them unchanged in the admin app. Orders already placed with the cake stay valid and are handled normally. The owner sees a confirmation naming the cake before removal. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-20 | Order window (Q-13, decided). Owner and staff see orders placed in the last 90 days, counted from the placed time. The owner is held to the same window because the owner has no task that needs older customer data (reports are a non-goal) and less visible personal data is safer. Older orders stay stored but are not shown, listed, searched or opened (exact edge in BR-23), with one exception (Q-22, changed by the owner): an order older than 90 days that is still New or Confirmed stays reachable, only from the Unfinished view (BR-27), so that staff can open it and close it as Completed or Cancelled with a reason (BR-16). It is not found by search and not reachable by a notification or any other way. Once closed it is older than the window and disappears for everyone. No automatic closing is done, because the shop did not decide that a system may complete or cancel an order by itself. [v1.4 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-21 | Ordering rules (Q-7, decided). Lead days and minimum cakes per order are owner settings; the booking horizon stays 30 days. A change applies to orders placed after it is saved and never alters or cancels an existing order, even one that no longer meets the new values. A customer order in progress that no longer meets the new values is refused by the server with the current rule, and the customer app shows the current values and lets the customer adjust. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-22 | Store-review account (Q-19, decided; ADR-0027 option B). A staff account with a fixed address and a fixed code, for Apple and Google reviewers who cannot receive a shop mail. It sees only fake demo orders (three, with fake names and numbers); real orders never appear for it and demo orders never appear for real accounts, never count toward the daily limit or the phone limit, and are never notified. It receives no notification and its settings or staff actions change no real data (it has staff rights, BR-1). It exists only while the operator has switched it on before submission; after approval it is switched off and the fixed code stops working. Every use is recorded. The code is never written in the repository or documents. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-23 | Exact boundaries. All times are server time, compared as instants (timestamps), never as calendar days. A code is valid while less than 10 minutes have passed since it was sent: at exactly 10:00 it is expired. A new code may be requested when at least 30 seconds have passed (exactly 30 s is allowed). The hourly limit looks back 60 minutes: the 4th code is allowed once the oldest of the 3 is at least 60 minutes old. A device is signed in while less than 7 days (168 hours) have passed since its last renewal: at exactly 7 days a code is needed. An order is shown while at most 90 days (2 160 hours) have passed since it was placed: at exactly 90 days it is still shown, one second later it is not. A revoked device gets no data after 5 minutes at most (BR-8). Delivery dates and "today" use the shop calendar day (A-1). [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-24 | Search (FR-11). The text is trimmed; it needs at least 3 characters (3 digits for a phone number), otherwise the search is not run and the screen says so. Matching is "contains", not case-sensitive, ignores Vietnamese tone marks and accents (so "tran thi" finds "Trần Thị", and "d" matches "đ"), and treats the same letters typed in different Unicode forms as equal. Order code: found with or without "BT-" and in any case. Phone: only digits count, spaces, dots, dashes and "+" are ignored, and "+84 900..." and "0900..." are the same number. Search covers only orders of the 90-day window and combines with the status and date filters. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-25 | Formats. E-mail address: after trimming, no spaces, exactly one "@" with text before and after it, a dot with text on both sides in the part after "@", at most 254 characters; stored and compared in lower case. Only the format is checked when an account is added, not that the mailbox exists. Contact phone: a Vietnamese number as the customer app accepts it (Place Order, ADR-0009: 10 digits starting with 0 followed by 3, 5, 7, 8 or 9, or the +84 or 84 form; spaces, dots and dashes ignored); whether landlines are allowed follows Place Order Q-O1. Zalo contact: such a phone number, or a Zalo account name of 3 to 100 characters. [v1.3 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-26 | New-device notice (Q-24, decided). After an owner sign-in by code on a device not in the owner's device list, one notice goes to the owner e-mail and the backup e-mail (only the owner e-mail if no backup is registered). It says that the owner account was signed in on a new device, the device name (phone model) and the time, and that the owner should revoke the device if it was not the owner. It contains no code, no customer data and no link that signs in. Renewals of an existing session send nothing. A recovery sign-in (BR-17) sends its own notice, not a second one. No notice for staff. [v1.4 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-27 | Unfinished orders (Q-22, changed by the owner). An order is unfinished when its status is New or Confirmed and its delivery date is today or earlier on the shop calendar (A-1), whatever its age. "Due today" means the delivery date is today; "overdue" means the date has passed. Both count in the badge. Seen by owner and staff alike (the store-review account sees only its demo orders). Rows show only the fields of FR-8 (code, customer name, status, date and slot, cakes, total, district); the phone and address appear only in the detail, as for every order (BR-15). Order of the view: BR-13. An order leaves the view as soon as it is Completed or Cancelled; a date later than today never counts. [v1.4 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-28 | Reminding without nagging. The badge (FR-49) and chip (FR-50) are quiet, always current and need no action. The banner (FR-51) appears at most once per opening, never as a modal, with no sound and a one-tap dismissal. There is no push reminder and no app-icon number by default (Q-23, Q-11): staff already get a push for every new order, and a daily push about old orders would be ignored. Recommended if the owner wants more: at most one push a day at a fixed shop time, only when the count is above 0, without customer data (text like "3 unfinished orders"). [v1.4 | 2026-10-31] |
