# Admin Orders: Business Rules (section 6)

Part of [Admin Orders](../admin-orders.md) (PRD v1.5). Holds: BR-1 to BR-14 (28 before). Most of what was a rule for users is now either one visible rule or an invisible system behaviour; invisible ones are marked "invisible". Values marked "proposed" are defaults the owner may change (change this document first, A-10). Place Order rules are cited as "PO BR-n". Every line is new or changed in v1.5 [v1.5 | 2026-10-31].

## 6. Business Rules

| ID | Rule |
| -- | ---- |
| BR-1 | Rights. Owner: everything. Staff (and the review account): sign in and out, list, search, open, confirm, complete and cancel orders, receive notifications, sign out own other phones. Only the owner: Settings (FR-20 to FR-24) and staff (FR-5). Neither role can edit an order, delete an order or see payment data. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-2 | Status changes, and no others: New to Confirmed, New to Cancelled, Confirmed to Completed, Confirmed to Cancelled. Completed and Cancelled are final. Cancelling returns the cakes to the remaining amount of the delivery date at once (PO BR-9); confirming or completing changes no capacity. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-3 | Record (invisible). The system stores, and nobody can edit or delete, every order creation and status change (old and new status, account name or "customer", time, cancel reason) and every settings change (account name, field, old and new value, time). Only the last change of an order is shown (FR-14); no history or audit screen (deferred). A deactivated staff member keeps the name in earlier records. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-4 | Codes (invisible, proposed values). A code has 6 digits, works once, and is valid for less than 10 minutes (at exactly 10:00 it is expired). A new code can be requested after 30 seconds and voids the old one; at most 3 codes per address per hour. The 5th wrong entry voids the current code. There is no time lock on the address (Q-20), and a signed-in phone is never affected. An unregistered address behaves the same and gets no mail. Only addresses the owner added can sign in; letter case and spaces around are ignored; an address needs "@" with text on both sides and a dot after it, at most 254 characters, and belongs to one account. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-5 | Sessions (invisible, Q-4, Q-15). A phone stays signed in; each use renews it for 7 days, so a phone used weekly never asks again; at 7 days without use a code is needed. Renewal credentials work once; an old one presented more than 20 seconds after replacement signs that phone out; within 20 seconds the repeat succeeds (ADR-0022). Access to data uses a credential of at most 5 minutes, which is why a removed phone loses data within 5 minutes. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-6 | Accounts (Q-5). One owner and up to 30 active staff; adding or switching on a staff member is refused at 30 with a message to switch one off first. The owner cannot be switched off. The review account (BR-14) is not counted. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-7 | Customer data (invisible). Visible only to a signed-in active account; not in notification text, logs, crash reports or analytics (the admin app has no analytics); removed from the phone at sign-out; hidden in the phone's app preview; not part of phone backup. Retention follows PO BR-12 and stays open (PO Q-O4). [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-8 | Cancel reasons (Q-9, list shortened by PROPOSAL P-4, Q-28). One required reason: customer asked, cannot deliver (also when the day is full), cannot reach customer, fake or spam order. Shown to staff and owner, never to the customer. A cancelled order does not count toward the 5-per-phone limit, except "fake or spam" (Q-12). [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-9 | What the list shows. Unfinished = New or Confirmed with delivery date today or earlier (shop calendar, Vietnam time). The default list holds all New and Confirmed orders of any date and any age, ordered by earliest delivery date, then earliest slot, then oldest placed. Completed and Cancelled orders are shown for 90 days after they were placed (Q-13, owner too; at exactly 90 days still shown), then not listed, searched or opened. No automatic closing. Orders load 50 at a time. The notification is only a prompt; the list is the truth. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-10 | Search. At least 3 characters (3 digits for a phone); "contains", ignoring case and accents ("tran" finds "Trần"); order code with or without "BT-"; phone digits only ("+84 900..." equals "0900..."); combines with filters. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-11 | Shop values (proposed ranges). Price: whole dong 1 to 100 000 000. Delivery fee: whole dong 1 to 10 000 000 (no free delivery, PO BR-3). Daily limit 1 to 9 999. Days of notice 0 to 14. Minimum cakes 1 to 20 and not above the daily limit, checked on the saved pair. Slot: start before end, no duplicate. Texts as in data-model section 3. Contact phone: a Vietnamese number as in PO ADR-0009; Zalo: such a number or an account name of 3 to 100 characters. All fields required; an invalid value is refused with a message naming the field and nothing of that form is saved. Changes apply to orders placed afterwards; existing orders keep their cake, price, fee, district and slot. Lowering the daily limit cancels nothing: dates above it show as full. The last active cake, district and slot cannot be switched off or removed. Two owner phones saving the same value: the last save wins. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-12 | Cakes. At most 12 cakes not removed (Q-17). Name 1 to 100 characters, unique; one image, JPEG or PNG, at most 5 MB (5 242 880 bytes, proposed); an iPhone HEIC photo is converted to JPEG on the phone first. Hidden cakes can be shown again; removed cakes cannot, are hidden from customers, and stay in earlier orders with their stored name and price. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-13 | New-device notice (invisible, Q-24, backup address part deferred). After an owner code sign-in on a phone the owner has not used before, one notice goes to the owner e-mail: owner account signed in on a new phone, phone model, time, advice to use "Sign out all other phones" if it was not the owner. No code, no link that signs in, no customer data. Not sent for renewals or staff. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-14 | Review account (invisible, Q-19). A staff account with a fixed address and code, on only while the operator switched it on for store review. It sees only three fake demo orders; real orders never appear for it, demo orders never for others, never count toward limits and never notify. The code is not written in the repository or documents. [v1.5 | 2026-10-31] |
