# Admin Orders: Business Rules (section 6)

Part of [Admin Orders](../admin-orders.md) (PRD v1.1). Holds: BR-1 to BR-21. Values marked "proposed" are defaults the owner may change (see [07-open-questions.md](07-open-questions.md)). Place Order rules (BR-1 to BR-13 there) are cited as "PO BR-n". Changed or new lines end with [v1.1 | 2026-10-31].

## 6. Business Rules

| ID | Rule |
| -- | ---- |
| BR-1 | Rights. Owner: everything. Staff: sign in and out, list, search, filter and open orders, confirm, complete and cancel orders, receive notifications, see and revoke own devices. Only the owner: settings (FR-22 to FR-26, FR-38 to FR-40), staff management (FR-27), backup e-mail and recovery (FR-42, FR-43), revoking other people's devices (FR-37). Neither role can edit an order's content, delete an order, or see payment data. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-2 | Allowed status changes, and no others: New to Confirmed, New to Cancelled, Confirmed to Completed, Confirmed to Cancelled. Completed may be set at any time once the order is Confirmed, also before the delivery date (Q-8, decided). Completed and Cancelled are final: no change out of them, no reopening. This follows the lifecycle already enforced by the database (docs/system/order-lifecycle.md section 6). [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-3 | Cancelling an order returns its cakes to the remaining amount of its delivery date at once (PO BR-9). Confirming or completing changes no capacity. |
| BR-4 | Every status change records who made it and when, and for a cancellation the reason (BR-16). The record cannot be edited or deleted. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-5 | One-time code: 6 digits, valid 10 minutes, usable once; a new code makes the old one invalid. A new code can be requested 30 seconds after the previous one. At most 3 codes per e-mail address per hour. After 5 wrong codes for one address the address is locked for 15 minutes. Limits stay although e-mail is cheap, to stop abuse and mail flooding of a third party. All values proposed. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-6 | Session (Q-4, decided). A device stays signed in for 7 days; each use within that time renews it for another 7 days (proposed reading, Q-15). A device not used for 7 days needs a new code. Signing out ends the session at once on that device. A renewal credential works once; presenting an already used one signs that device out (FR-36). [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-7 | Only an e-mail address that the owner added can sign in. Addresses are compared without regard to upper or lower case. An address belongs to one account, and the backup e-mail of the owner cannot also be an account address. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-8 | Accounts (Q-5, decided): exactly one owner and up to 30 active staff. The owner cannot deactivate or delete the owner account. A deactivated staff account keeps its name in the status history of earlier orders. When the owner deactivates an account or revokes a device, the device loses access to data within 5 minutes (proposed bound; exact lifetimes are the architect's, TQ-4) and cannot renew its sign-in from that moment. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-9 | Setting limits. Cake price: whole dong, 1 to 100 000 000. Delivery fee: whole dong, at least 1 (PO BR-3, no free delivery), at most 10 000 000. Daily limit: whole number from 1 to 9 999. Lead days: whole number from 0 to 14 (proposed). Minimum cakes per order: whole number from 1 to 20 and not above the daily limit (proposed). Slot: start earlier than end, no two slots with the same start and end, same list on every date (PO BR-10, BR-11). Name and description lengths: as in docs/system/data-model section 3 (name 1 to 100, description 1 to 300, greeting 1 to 300, district name 1 to 100, unique). Contact phone: valid phone number; Zalo: as the customer app uses it. All fields are required. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-10 | Lowering the daily limit below the cakes already accepted for a date cancels nothing: the existing orders stay, that date shows as full to customers, and cancelling orders on it frees room again. Before saving, the owner is told which dates are already above the new limit (count and dates). Raising the limit makes room at once. |
| BR-11 | Cakes. The owner can add, hide and remove cakes (Q-6, decided). A hidden cake can be shown again; a removed cake cannot. At least one cake must stay active (PO BR-13 treats zero active cakes as an error state of the customer app), so the last active cake cannot be hidden or removed. Likewise the last active district and the last active slot cannot be deactivated. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-12 | Price and fee changes follow PO BR-5 and BR-6: a customer who has an order open on the review step with the old price is asked to confirm the new total (PO behaviour "price changed"); stored orders never change. Hidden or removed cakes, deactivated districts and slots disappear from the customer app at its next refresh and are refused by the server at once. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-13 | List order and size. Default list: New and Confirmed orders, earliest delivery date first, then earliest slot, then oldest placed first. With status Completed or Cancelled in the filter, the same order applies. The list loads 50 orders at a time and loads the next 50 when the person reaches the end. |
| BR-14 | Notification content and reach: see FR-31, FR-32. The notification is a prompt, not the record: the list is always the source of truth, and an order whose notification was lost is still in the list. |
| BR-15 | Customer data in the admin app is visible only to a signed-in active account, is not written to the notification text, logs or analytics, and is removed from the device when the person signs out. Retention of the stored data follows PO BR-12 (period still pending, PO Q-O4); the app shows only the window of BR-20. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-16 | Cancel reasons (Q-9, decided). The reason is required and is one of: customer asked, day full, cannot deliver, cannot reach customer, fake or spam order. The reason is shown to staff and owner, not to the customer. A cancelled order does not count toward the limit of 5 orders per phone number per 24 hours (Q-12, decided), except a "fake or spam order" cancellation, which keeps counting. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-17 | Owner recovery (Q-14, decided, two-code form proposed in Q-16). Recovery sign-in on a new device needs two different codes, one sent to the owner e-mail and one to the backup e-mail, both valid under BR-5. Without a registered backup e-mail the recovery is not offered and the operator recovers the account outside the app (A-7). The owner is reminded in the app to register a backup e-mail until it is set. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-18 | New cake data. Name 1 to 100 characters and unique among cakes not removed; description 1 to 300; price as in BR-9; exactly one image, JPEG or PNG, up to 5 MB (proposed). At most 12 cakes not removed, active or hidden (proposed, Q-17). [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-19 | Removing a cake. Removed means hidden from the customer app, refused for new orders, and kept in the database for history. Earlier orders keep the cake name, quantity and price as stored and show them unchanged in the admin app. Orders already placed with the cake stay valid and are handled normally. The owner sees a confirmation naming the cake before removal. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-20 | Order window (Q-13, decided). Owner and staff see orders placed in the last 90 days, counted from the placed time. The owner is held to the same window because the owner has no task that needs older customer data (reports are a non-goal) and less visible personal data is safer. Older orders stay stored but are not shown, listed, searched or opened. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
| BR-21 | Ordering rules (Q-7, decided). Lead days and minimum cakes per order are owner settings; the booking horizon stays 30 days. A change applies to orders placed after it is saved and never alters or cancels an existing order, even one that no longer meets the new values. A customer order in progress that no longer meets the new values is refused by the server with the current rule, and the customer app shows the current values and lets the customer adjust. [v1.1 | 2026-10-31] |
